Privacy Policy

1. Introduction

CycleVia: Period Tracker (“CycleVia,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) is developed by MattaSantoshi. We recognize that menstrual and reproductive health information is sensitive. This Privacy Policy describes the information the app may collect and process, why it is used, how it may be stored, and how you can contact us about privacy matters.

By using CycleVia, you acknowledge this policy. Where required by law, we seek consent for processing sensitive personal information.

2. Information you provide

Depending on the features you use, information may include:

  • Account information: email address, account identifiers, profile details, and authentication-related information.
  • Cycle and health entries: period start and end dates, cycle duration, symptoms, mood, wellness observations, personal notes, and other information you choose to record.
  • Preferences: reminder preferences and app settings.
  • Subscription information: purchase identifiers, subscription status, and entitlement information where paid plans are enabled.

3. Technical information

As necessary to operate and secure the app, technical services may process device platform, app version, session identifiers, security events, and diagnostic information. The precise categories depend on the services enabled in the released version.

4. How information is used

  • To create, authenticate, and manage accounts.
  • To record and display cycle history and personal tracking entries.
  • To calculate estimated upcoming period dates and related cycle insights.
  • To support optional reminders and user preferences.
  • To provide subscription entitlements where applicable.
  • To maintain security, resolve problems, respond to support requests, and comply with applicable law.

We do not sell menstrual health information to data brokers. We do not use sensitive menstrual health information for unrelated advertising or profiling without an appropriate legal basis and any consent required by law.

5. Health information and predictions

Important: CycleVia is a wellness and tracking tool, not a medical device or a substitute for professional medical advice. Predictions are estimates, not guarantees. Do not rely on period or fertility estimates as contraception, pregnancy confirmation, or medical diagnosis. Consult a qualified healthcare professional about health concerns.

6. Cloud storage and Supabase

CycleVia uses Supabase backend services. Account information and user-entered menstrual tracking information may be stored in a cloud database. Supabase may support authentication, database operations, and related infrastructure. Cloud storage may involve processing outside your country of residence. Access restrictions and security measures depend on the app’s actual deployed configuration.

7. Service providers and sharing

Information may be processed by providers needed to operate the app, such as Supabase for backend services and Google Play or Apple for store distribution and applicable purchases. If enabled, a subscription-management provider such as RevenueCat may process purchase or entitlement identifiers. Additional analytics, crash-reporting, advertising, or notification providers, if used, must be disclosed in this policy before release.

Information may also be disclosed where legally required or necessary to address security or fraud. We do not publicly disclose your private cycle entries as part of the tracking functionality.

8. Consent and legal basis

Where required by applicable law, we rely on consent, performance of requested services, compliance with legal obligations, or other permitted grounds. You may withdraw consent where applicable, although some features may then be unavailable.

9. Data retention

Account and cycle information may be retained while an account remains active. After a verified deletion request, eligible personal information will be deleted or anonymized according to implemented procedures and applicable legal requirements. Limited records may be retained where required for legal, transaction, fraud-prevention, or security reasons.

10. Security

We aim to use reasonable technical and organizational safeguards appropriate to the information processed. No system is completely secure. Please protect your account credentials and contact us if you suspect unauthorized access.

11. Your choices and privacy rights

Depending on applicable law, you may request access, correction, deletion, withdrawal of consent, or information about how your personal data is processed. Contact cycleviaperiodtracker@gmail.com. We may verify your identity before fulfilling requests.

12. Notifications and permissions

If the app requests notification or other device permissions, you may manage them through your device settings. Permissions should be requested only for features that need them.

13. Children's privacy

CycleVia’s eligibility requirements and any protections for minors must match the age groups and safeguards configured in the released app and its store listings.
